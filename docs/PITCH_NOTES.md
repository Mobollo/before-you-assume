# Pitch notes

## One sentence

Before You Assume helps diverse urban communities understand one another, navigate everyday social friction and prevent misunderstandings from becoming conflict.

## The insight

People share physical spaces without necessarily sharing the same unwritten social rules.

## Why now

Urban communities are becoming more diverse and interconnected. The cost of misunderstanding is often social rather than technological: mistrust, repeated friction and weakened relationships.

## What makes the approach different

The product does not try to make everyone behave the same way. It creates room for perspective, local verification and constructive action.

## Prototype scope

- New resident orientation
- Everyday incident perspective tool
- Community leader pulse dashboard

## Future potential

The concept could be adapted for estates, informal settlements, community associations, campuses and other urban communities across African cities.

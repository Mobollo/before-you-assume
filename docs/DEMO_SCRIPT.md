# Demo script

## Opening — the problem

A new neighbour arrives. He sees a wire on an electricity pole and uses it without asking. The person who left the wire there sees the action as crossing a boundary.

Neither person necessarily set out to create conflict. They acted from different assumptions.

The question behind Before You Assume is simple:

> What if people had a practical way to understand the community they were entering — and to pause and consider another perspective when friction occurs?

## Journey 1 — Know Your Community

A new resident opens the community guide and sees practical, locally relevant information about shared spaces, communication, community contacts and possible areas of misunderstanding.

## Journey 2 — Before You Assume

The resident describes an incident. The tool separates what happened from interpretation, surfaces possible perspectives, identifies what needs to be clarified and suggests constructive next steps.

It does not declare a winner.

## Journey 3 — Community Pulse

A community leader sees anonymised recurring themes from resident feedback and can identify areas where a conversation or clearer community guidance may help.

## Closing

> Different people. Shared understanding. Better coexistence.

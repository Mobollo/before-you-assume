Before You Assume

Example incident

“My neighbour used a wire I had left on the electricity pole without asking.”

Your perspective: You may see this as someone crossing a property boundary or using something you had reserved.
Another possible perspective: The neighbour may have interpreted an apparently unused wire as available or communal.

What needs clarification?

  • Who owns the wire?
  • What was it intended for?
  • What does the community normally expect around shared electrical infrastructure?

# Before You Assume

### Helping diverse urban communities understand one another better.

**OSF × Andela Hackathon 2026**  
**Track:** Stability & Social Cohesion  
**Country:** Nigeria

Before You Assume is a civic-tech proof of concept for helping people in diverse urban communities understand differences, navigate everyday friction and prevent misunderstandings from becoming conflict.

## The problem

People moving into cities and neighbourhoods often bring different assumptions about respect, boundaries, communication, ownership and community life. These differences are normal. The difficulty begins when those assumptions remain unspoken and people interpret unfamiliar behaviour without context.

## The three users

- **New residents:** understand the community they are entering.
- **Existing residents:** understand unfamiliar behaviour before judging it.
- **Community leaders:** identify recurring sources of friction and respond early.

## Core experience

1. **Know Your Community** — a practical orientation for new residents.
2. **Before You Assume** — guided perspective-taking around an everyday incident.
3. **Community Pulse** — anonymised themes that help leaders see recurring friction.

## Design principles

- Different people do not need to think alike to live well together.
- AI should help people understand differences, not decide who is right.
- Local information should be verified before it is presented as community guidance.
- Privacy matters, especially around conflict reports.
- The experience should work in low-bandwidth environments and remain simple.
- Cultural information should never be treated as a stereotype or universal rule.

## AI use

AI tools may support incident description, perspective-taking, translation and simplification, theme identification from anonymised feedback, and suggestions for constructive next steps. AI should not invent community rules, label individuals by culture or determine who is right.

## Repository structure

- `prototype/` — the current browser-based proof of concept
- `docs/` — problem framing, trust model, demo script and pitch notes
- `assets/` — presentation/demo assets as they are created

## Running the prototype

No build system is required for the first prototype. Open `prototype/index.html` in a browser, or serve the repository with any simple local web server.

## Status
